Legal · Data handling

Privacy Policy

A plain account of what Stay Fit stores, what it reads from Apple Health or Health Connect, and — the short version — what it never does: send your data anywhere we control, sell it, or hand it to advertisers.

Stored on your device

Every workout, meal, water and sleep entry lives in a local database on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to a server we run — because we don't run one.

Health sync is optional

Apple Health / Health Connect only exchange data if you turn sync on, and only for the metrics you approve in your device's own permission screen.

No ads, no data sale

There is no advertising SDK or analytics tracker in this app, and no third party is ever paid for or given your personal information.

01 Information we collect

Stay Fit asks you for a few details when you set up your profile, and records what you log as you use the app. All of it is described here plainly — there's no hidden collection beyond what's listed.

CategoryExamplesWhere it's stored
ProfileName, age, gender, height, weight, target weight, bioOn device
Workout activityRoutines, exercises, sets, reps, weights, session historyOn device
NutritionLogged meals, calories, macros, saved foodsOn device
RecoveryWater intake, sleep duration, bedtime / wake timeOn device
Health metrics (optional)Steps, active energy, sleep, workouts from Apple Health / Health ConnectOn device

All of the above is stored in a local database on your phone using standard on-device storage. We do not operate a server that this data is sent to, and Stay Fit does not require you to create an account.

02 Apple Health & Health Connect

If you turn on health sync in Settings, Stay Fit asks the operating system for permission to read specific metrics — steps, active energy, sleep, and workouts. You approve exactly what's shared in Apple's or Google's own permission screen, not ours, and you can revoke it at any time from your device's Health app or Health Connect settings.

We only read. Stay Fit does not write data back into Apple Health or Health Connect, and the data it reads is used to fill in your in-app totals — it is never uploaded anywhere else.

03 Food lookup (USDA FoodData Central)

The food search feature sends the text you type (e.g. "chicken breast") to the United States Department of Agriculture's public FoodData Central API to look up calories and macros. This request contains only the search text — no name, profile information, or device identifier is included or ever sent.

04 How your information is used

  • Calculating your BMI, macro targets, and progress trends
  • Showing your logged history back to you across the app
  • Filling in daily totals automatically when health sync is enabled
  • Generating the CSV file when you use the in-app data export feature

Your information is never used to build an advertising profile, and we do not have an analytics or advertising SDK integrated into Stay Fit to do so.

05 Storage & security

Your data is stored in an encrypted-at-rest, sandboxed local database, protected the same way the rest of your phone's app data is protected by iOS or Android. Because nothing leaves your device under normal use, there is no separate server-side breach surface for this information — the security of your data is tied to the security of your device.

06 Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only outside systems Stay Fit talks to are:

  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — only if you opt in, and only to read the metrics you approve
  • USDA FoodData Central — only your typed search text, for food lookup

Neither is paid for your data, and neither receives anything beyond what's described above.

07 Your choices

  • Health sync — turn it on or off anytime from Profile → Health Sync, or revoke access entirely from your device's system settings
  • Units — switch between metric and imperial without affecting what's stored
  • Export — download a CSV of everything you've logged from Profile → Export / Backup Data

08 Deleting your data

Uninstalling Stay Fit permanently deletes its local database along with it — there is no copy stored anywhere else to separately request the deletion of. If you'd like to keep using the app but clear specific data first, the delete controls throughout the app (routines, logged sets, meals) remove that entry immediately and permanently.

09 Children's privacy

Stay Fit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group.

10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Meaningful changes — anything that changes what we collect or how it's used — will also be called out in the app's release notes.
