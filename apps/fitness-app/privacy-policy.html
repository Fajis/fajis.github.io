Privacy Policy

A plain account of what Stay Fit stores, what it reads from (and saves to) Apple Health or Health Connect, and exactly when anything leaves your phone — only when you use Ask AI or the weekly review, search for or scan a food, or buy Premium, plus an anonymous report if the app crashes. We never sell your data or hand it to advertisers.

Stored on your device

Every workout, meal, water, sleep and health entry, and every progress photo, lives on your phone. There's no account, and no server of ours keeps a copy.

You choose what's shared

Health sync only reads what you approve in your device's permission screen, and Ask AI only uses your data if you allow it.

No ads, no data sale

There is no advertising SDK or analytics tracker in this app, only an anonymous crash reporter, and your personal information is never sold or used for advertising.

01 Information we collect

Stay Fit asks you for a few details when you set up your profile, and records what you log as you use the app. All of it is described here plainly — there's no hidden collection beyond what's listed.

All of the above is stored in a local database on your phone, and Stay Fit does not require you to create an account. Some of it is sent off your device only in the cases described in sections 04–06: when you use Ask AI or the weekly review, search for or scan a food, or buy Premium.

02 Apple Health & Health Connect

If you turn on health sync in Profile, Stay Fit asks the operating system for permission to read specific data — steps, active energy, sleep, workouts (including distance and heart rate recorded during them), resting heart rate, heart rate variability and VO2 max. You approve exactly what's shared in Apple's or Google's own permission screen, not ours, and you can revoke it at any time from your device's Health app or Health Connect settings.

On Android, Stay Fit also asks permission to save your workouts to Health Connect. When it's allowed, each workout you log in Stay Fit is added there as a strength training session with its name, start and end time, and a short list of the exercises and sets you did, so other fitness apps you've connected can see it. Editing or deleting the workout in Stay Fit updates or removes it there too. You can turn this off at any time by removing Stay Fit's permission to write exercise in Health Connect.

Imported workouts and daily figures are saved in the app's local database so your history and trends load instantly and build up over time. This is used to show your cardio sessions, heart and recovery trends, and readiness score inside the app.

It stays on your phone. The only thing Stay Fit writes is your own workouts into Health Connect, on your phone, with your permission. Health data is never uploaded to any server, never sent to Ask AI, and never used for advertising.

03 Camera & photos

Stay Fit uses your camera for two things only, and only while you have that screen open: scanning a food barcode and taking a progress photo. Android asks your permission the first time, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.

  • Barcode scanning: the barcode is read on your phone. The camera picture is never saved or sent anywhere; only the barcode number is used to look up the product (see section 05).
  • Progress photos: photos you take, or pick from your gallery, are copied into Stay Fit's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, never sent to Ask AI, and other apps can't see them. When you pick from your gallery, Android's photo picker gives Stay Fit only the photo you choose, not your whole library.
  • Share cards: when you share a workout, record or monthly recap, the image is made on your phone and goes only where you choose in your phone's share menu.

04 Ask AI & weekly review

Ask AI answers fitness, nutrition and recovery questions using an AI model provided by Groq. It's optional, and nothing is sent until you've seen a screen explaining what's shared and made a choice.

  • Always sent when you ask: your question and the earlier messages in that chat.
  • Also sent, only if you allow personalised answers: your name, age, gender, height, weight, target weight and BMI; your workout streak, total workouts logged and your last 5 workouts (date, name, duration and volume); and today's calories and your calorie target.
  • Weekly review (Premium), only if you allow personalised answers: for each of the last 7 days, your total calories and protein, your workouts (name, duration and volume), water and hours of sleep; plus your goals (calories, protein, water, sleep, target weight and date), your weight change that week and your workout streak. The finished review is saved on your phone.
  • Never sent: individual meals or food names, progress photos, body measurements, exercise notes, or any Apple Health / Health Connect data.

Requests pass through a small server Stay Fit runs on Cloudflare, which holds the AI provider's key so it isn't stored in the app. That server forwards your request to Groq and returns the answer; it does not store your questions or data, and keeps only a count of requests per day, overall and per IP address, to enforce usage limits. These counts hold no questions or other data and are deleted automatically after two days. As the host, Cloudflare handles basic connection data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Groq processes the request to generate the answer under its own privacy policy. Chats are not saved — closing Ask AI clears the conversation.

You can turn personalised answers on or off at any time with the “Personalised” setting at the top of the Ask AI screen. Ask AI is a general assistant, not medical advice.

05 Food search & barcodes

The food search feature sends the text you type (e.g. "chicken breast") to the United States Department of Agriculture's public FoodData Central API to look up calories and macros. This request contains only the search text — no name, profile information, or device identifier is included or ever sent.

When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, non-profit food database, to find the product. The request names the app (Stay Fit) as the sender, as Open Food Facts asks, but contains nothing about you. Open Food Facts handles it under its own privacy policy.

06 Premium purchases

Stay Fit Premium is sold through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment — Stay Fit never sees your card or billing details. To check whether your subscription is active and restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service.

RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription status from the store, and basic device information such as platform and app version. It does not receive any of your workout, nutrition or health data. RevenueCat handles this under its own privacy policy.

07 Crash reports

If Stay Fit crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that helps us find and fix bugs. A report contains the error itself, where in the app's code it happened, and basic device information: phone model, operating system and app version.

Crash reports do not include your name, email, workouts, meals, weight, health or heart data, or photos. The app doesn't attach any user ID, doesn't record what you tap or type, and Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. Sentry handles reports under its own privacy policy.

08 How your information is used

  • Calculating your BMI, macro targets, readiness score and progress trends
  • Showing your logged history — and imported health workouts and trends — back to you across the app
  • Filling in daily totals automatically when health sync is enabled
  • Answering Ask AI questions, personalised with your data only if you allow it
  • Checking whether your Premium subscription is active
  • Showing your progress photos, body measurements and fasting history back to you
  • Reminders and the "fast complete" alert, which are scheduled on your phone
  • Generating the CSV file when you use the in-app data export feature
  • Finding and fixing bugs, using anonymous crash reports

Your information is never used to build an advertising profile, and we do not have an analytics or advertising SDK integrated into Stay Fit to do so. Sentry, described in section 07, only receives anonymous crash reports.

09 Storage & security

Your data is stored in an encrypted-at-rest, sandboxed local database, protected the same way the rest of your phone's app data is protected by iOS or Android. Stay Fit has no server that stores your logs or health data, so there is no server-side copy of ours to breach — the security of your data is tied to the security of your device and your own device backups. Data sent for Ask AI, the weekly review, food search, barcode lookup, purchases or crash reports travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Device backups. On Android, your phone's built-in backup may include Stay Fit's data, including progress photos, in your device backup to your own Google account, so your history comes back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is encrypted and managed by Google as part of your account — Stay Fit can't access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

10 Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only outside services Stay Fit talks to are:

  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — only if you opt in: to read the data you approve, and on Android to save your workouts to Health Connect
  • Groq, via Stay Fit's server on Cloudflare — your Ask AI questions, plus the summaries in section 04 only if you allow it
  • USDA FoodData Central — only your typed search text, for food lookup
  • Open Food Facts — only a scanned barcode number, for barcode lookup
  • Google Play / App Store and RevenueCat — to process and verify Premium purchases, as described in section 06. The app may also ask Google Play or the App Store to show its rating window; anything you submit there goes to them, not to us
  • Sentry — anonymous crash reports only, as described in section 07

None of these is paid for your data, and none receives anything beyond what's described above.

11 Your choices

  • Health sync — turn it on or off anytime from Profile → Health Sync, or revoke access entirely from your device's system settings
  • Ask AI data sharing — turn personalised answers on or off with the “Personalised” setting at the top of Ask AI. With it off, only your questions are sent
  • Camera & photos — allow or deny camera access when asked, or change it later in your phone's settings. Barcode scanning and progress photos simply won't work without it; everything else does
  • Units — switch between metric and imperial without affecting what's stored
  • Export — download a CSV of everything you've logged from Profile → Export / Backup Data

12 Deleting your data

Uninstalling Stay Fit deletes its local database along with it, and Stay Fit keeps no copy of your logs anywhere else. If your phone's device backup is on (see section 09), a copy may remain in your own Google account's backup until you delete it there or turn backup off. If you'd like to keep using the app but clear specific data first, the delete controls throughout the app (routines, logged sets, meals, progress photos, measurements, fasts) remove that entry immediately and permanently, and Profile → Health Sync → Delete imported health data removes everything copied from Apple Health or Health Connect (the originals there aren't touched). Workouts Stay Fit saved to Health Connect can be deleted there, or by deleting the workout in Stay Fit.

Crash reports hold nothing that identifies you, so they can't be matched back to you to delete on request; Sentry deletes them automatically within 90 days.

Ask AI conversations aren't stored by Stay Fit. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store and by RevenueCat as needed for billing; to ask about removing the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous ID, contact us below.

13 Children's privacy

Stay Fit is made for adults aged 18 and over, and the app doesn't let anyone younger set up a profile. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
