Legal · Data handling

Privacy Policy

A plain account of what Stay Fit stores, what it reads from Apple Health or Health Connect, and exactly when anything leaves your phone — only when you use Ask AI, search for a food, or buy Premium. We never sell your data or hand it to advertisers.

Stored on your device

Every workout, meal, water, sleep and health entry lives in a local database on your phone. There's no account, and no server of ours keeps a copy.

You choose what's shared

Health sync only reads what you approve in your device's permission screen, and Ask AI only uses your data if you allow it.

No ads, no data sale

There is no advertising SDK or analytics tracker in this app, and your personal information is never sold or used for advertising.

01 Information we collect

Stay Fit asks you for a few details when you set up your profile, and records what you log as you use the app. All of it is described here plainly — there's no hidden collection beyond what's listed.

CategoryExamplesWhere it's stored
ProfileName, age, gender, height, weight, target weight, bioOn device
Workout activityRoutines, exercises, sets, reps, weights, session historyOn device
NutritionLogged meals, calories, macros, saved foodsOn device
RecoveryWater intake, sleep duration, bedtime / wake timeOn device
Health data (optional)Steps, active energy, sleep; workouts with their type, duration, distance, calories and heart rate; resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) and VO2 max — from Apple Health / Health ConnectOn device
SettingsUnits, reminders, your Ask AI data-sharing choice, cached Premium statusOn device

All of the above is stored in a local database on your phone, and Stay Fit does not require you to create an account. Some of it is sent off your device only in the cases described in sections 03–05: when you ask Ask AI a question, search for a food, or buy Premium.

02 Apple Health & Health Connect

If you turn on health sync in Profile, Stay Fit asks the operating system for permission to read specific data — steps, active energy, sleep, workouts (including distance and heart rate recorded during them), resting heart rate, heart rate variability and VO2 max. You approve exactly what's shared in Apple's or Google's own permission screen, not ours, and you can revoke it at any time from your device's Health app or Health Connect settings.

Imported workouts and daily figures are saved in the app's local database so your history and trends load instantly and build up over time. This is used to show your cardio sessions, heart and recovery trends, and readiness score inside the app.

We only read, and it stays on your phone. Stay Fit does not write data back into Apple Health or Health Connect. Health data is never uploaded to any server, never sent to Ask AI, and never used for advertising.

03 Ask AI

Ask AI answers fitness, nutrition and recovery questions using an AI model provided by Groq. It's optional, and nothing is sent until you've seen a screen explaining what's shared and made a choice.

  • Always sent when you ask: your question and the earlier messages in that chat.
  • Also sent, only if you allow personalised answers: your name, age, gender, height, weight, target weight and BMI; your workout streak, total workouts logged and your last 5 workouts (date, name, duration and volume); and today's calories and your calorie target.
  • Never sent: individual meals, water, sleep, or any Apple Health / Health Connect data.

Requests pass through a small server Stay Fit runs on Cloudflare, which holds the AI provider's key so it isn't stored in the app. That server forwards your request to Groq and returns the answer; it does not store your questions or data, and keeps only an anonymous count of requests per day to enforce a usage limit. As the host, Cloudflare handles basic connection data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Groq processes the request to generate the answer under its own privacy policy. Chats are not saved — closing Ask AI clears the conversation.

You can turn personalised answers on or off at any time in Profile → Data & Legal → Share my data with Ask AI. Ask AI is a general assistant, not medical advice.

04 Food lookup (USDA FoodData Central)

The food search feature sends the text you type (e.g. "chicken breast") to the United States Department of Agriculture's public FoodData Central API to look up calories and macros. This request contains only the search text — no name, profile information, or device identifier is included or ever sent.

05 Premium purchases

Stay Fit Premium is sold through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment — Stay Fit never sees your card or billing details. To check whether your subscription is active and restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service.

RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription status from the store, and basic device information such as platform and app version. It does not receive any of your workout, nutrition or health data. RevenueCat handles this under its own privacy policy.

06 How your information is used

  • Calculating your BMI, macro targets, readiness score and progress trends
  • Showing your logged history — and imported health workouts and trends — back to you across the app
  • Filling in daily totals automatically when health sync is enabled
  • Answering Ask AI questions, personalised with your data only if you allow it
  • Checking whether your Premium subscription is active
  • Generating the CSV file when you use the in-app data export feature

Your information is never used to build an advertising profile, and we do not have an analytics or advertising SDK integrated into Stay Fit to do so.

07 Storage & security

Your data is stored in an encrypted-at-rest, sandboxed local database, protected the same way the rest of your phone's app data is protected by iOS or Android. Stay Fit has no server that stores your logs or health data, so there is no separate server-side copy to breach — the security of your data is tied to the security of your device. Data sent for Ask AI, food search or purchases travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

08 Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only outside services Stay Fit talks to are:

  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — only if you opt in, and only to read the data you approve
  • Groq, via Stay Fit's server on Cloudflare — your Ask AI questions, plus the profile, workout and calorie summary in section 03 only if you allow it
  • USDA FoodData Central — only your typed search text, for food lookup
  • Google Play / App Store and RevenueCat — to process and verify Premium purchases, as described in section 05

None of these is paid for your data, and none receives anything beyond what's described above.

09 Your choices

  • Health sync — turn it on or off anytime from Profile → Health Sync, or revoke access entirely from your device's system settings
  • Ask AI data sharing — turn personalised answers on or off in Profile → Data & Legal. With it off, only your questions are sent
  • Units — switch between metric and imperial without affecting what's stored
  • Export — download a CSV of everything you've logged from Profile → Export / Backup Data

10 Deleting your data

Uninstalling Stay Fit permanently deletes its local database along with it — there is no copy of your logs stored anywhere else to separately request the deletion of. If you'd like to keep using the app but clear specific data first, the delete controls throughout the app (routines, logged sets, meals) remove that entry immediately and permanently, and Profile → Health Sync → Delete imported health data removes everything copied from Apple Health or Health Connect (the originals there aren't touched).

Ask AI conversations aren't stored by Stay Fit. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store and by RevenueCat as needed for billing; to ask about removing the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous ID, contact us below.

11 Children's privacy

Stay Fit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group.
