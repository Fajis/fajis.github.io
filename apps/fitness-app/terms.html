02 What Stay Fit is

Stay Fit helps you log workouts, meals, water, sleep and body progress, see trends, and ask an AI assistant fitness and nutrition questions. Some features are free and some need Premium (section 05).

Numbers the app shows you, such as calorie and macro targets, BMI, the readiness score and food nutrition values, are estimates. They are based on the details you enter, on general formulas, and on public food databases, so they can be off. Use them as a guide, not as a measurement.

03 Your health & safety

Not medical advice. Stay Fit and Ask AI give general fitness and nutrition information. They are not a doctor, dietitian or physical therapist, and nothing in the app is a diagnosis or treatment.
  • Check with a doctor before you start or change an exercise or eating plan, especially if you have a health condition, an injury, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating.
  • Exercise carries a risk of injury. You choose what to do and how hard to push, and you do it at your own risk. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or anything unusual.
  • Use the exercise pictures and instructions as a reminder, not a replacement for proper coaching. Lift weights that suit you and use a spotter where it makes sense.
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Don't rely on the app.
